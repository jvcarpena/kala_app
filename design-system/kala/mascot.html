Meet Kala, your koala companion

Drop the "o" from koala and you get Kala. It's the friend who sits next to your to-do list: happy when you finish things, sleepy when you should be, and never judgy when you forget. Kala ko tapos na?

Kala

Kala: kala ko tapos na, for every time you were sure it was done.

PersonalityChill, warm, a little makakalimutin (forgetful) too. Koalas sleep 20 hours a day, so Kala gets it.
JobMakes the app feel like company. It reacts to what you do, but it never blocks you and never nags.
LookSoft grey fur, the big dark koala nose, and rosy cheeks. Flat shapes with no outlines, to match the app.
Color ruleKala itself is neutral. Props take the color of the domain you're in: a blue headband for Work, a violet book for Study, a green barbell for Health.

Poses

Nine moods, each tied to a moment in the app. They're all built from one SVG rig (same head, body and nose, with swappable eyes, mouth, arms and props), so in React Native each pose is just props on one <Kala /> component.

In the app

Kala shows up in a few set places, small and out of the way, and only when there's something to react to.

App icon and wordmark

kalakala ko tapos na

Kala's face sits inside the Day Ring, so the icon ties the mascot to the main idea of the app. The ring arcs use the four domain colors.

Kala's voice

Light Taglish by default, switchable to English in Settings. Short and kind, with one line at a time. Kala jokes about forgetting but never about the user's effort.

Rules for Kala

Never in the wayKala never covers a control, never needs a tap to dismiss, and never shows up more than once per screen.
Kind about forgettingThe "kala ko" joke is about the moment, not the person. Every oops line comes with a fix (Move, Mark done).
You can turn it downSettings → Companion: Chatty, Quiet (poses only, no lines) or Off. Language: Taglish or English.
Calm motionOne small bounce when Kala appears, with nothing looping. Reduced motion shows Kala without the bounce.
AccessibleKala is decorative (hidden from screen readers), and anything Kala says is real text in the bubble, which screen readers do read.
Works offlineKala is drawn with code (react-native-svg), not image files, so it's tiny, sharp at any size, and themes with dark mode.
